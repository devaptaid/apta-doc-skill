# Executive Summary

This illustrative proposal shows how Aptaworks structures a reporting modernization project. Contoso is a fictional client used to demonstrate the document format. The scope, milestones, and acceptance criteria below are examples for discussion.

> **Recommendation:** start with one reporting domain, validate the results with its business owner, and use the agreed approach for subsequent domains.

## Target Outcomes

- A shared definition for the selected operational metrics.
- A repeatable reporting workflow with documented ownership.
- Quality checks that make discrepancies visible before publication.
- Practical guidance that helps the team operate the workflow independently.

### Pilot Acceptance Criteria

The pilot is accepted when the agreed source data is reconciled, the report matches the approved metric definitions, and the business owner signs off on the operating procedure. Targets and baselines will be confirmed during discovery.

## Delivery Workplan

| Phase | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Confirm users, sources, and definitions | Approved scope and acceptance criteria |
| Pilot | Build and validate one reporting workflow | Tested pipeline and reviewed report |
| Adoption | Transfer ownership and document operation | Runbook and team walkthrough |

## Delivery Principles

1. Keep source data and business definitions traceable.
2. Test quality at each handoff in the workflow.
3. Record assumptions and decisions as they are made.

```text
Source -> Validation -> Transformation -> Reporting -> Review
```

## Next Steps

Schedule a discovery session to confirm the pilot domain, decision owner, available data, and acceptance criteria. Review the workplan and commercial scope after those inputs have been agreed.
